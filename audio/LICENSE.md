## Kenney

The short interface and foley samples in `samples/` come from these Kenney packs:

- UI Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio
- Interface Sounds: https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
- Impact Sounds: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- RPG Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
- Sci-Fi Sounds: https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds
- Licence: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Files were renamed, trimmed, mixed or otherwise adapted for their use in this portfolio. Kenney does not require attribution, but is credited here for clarity.

Clickable-world files made from Kenney samples:

- `bulb-pop.ogg`, `axe-pull.ogg`, `watch-tap.ogg`, `server-alarm.ogg`: Interface Sounds, with Impact Sounds also used in `axe-pull.ogg`.
- `post-thump-1.ogg`, `post-thump-2.ogg`, `wood-stack-1.ogg`, `wood-stack-2.ogg`, `pegboard-lift-1.ogg`, `pegboard-lift-2.ogg`: Impact Sounds.
- `sign-spin.ogg`: adapted from the existing Kenney `creak-1.ogg` and `creak-2.ogg` samples.
- `server-spinup.ogg`: Sci-Fi Sounds.

Daypart-idle files made from Kenney samples:

- `build-pass.ogg`, `call-join.ogg`: Interface Sounds (`confirmation_001.ogg` and `confirmation_004.ogg`).
